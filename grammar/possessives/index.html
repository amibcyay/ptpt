Possessives — meu or o meu?

European Portuguese often puts a definite article before possessives: o meu livro. Sometimes the article drops. Both can be correct — the difference is emphasis and structure.

Default EP pattern
  • Before a noun (determiner): usually article + possessive — as minhas chaves, o nosso carro.
  • Standing alone as “mine / yours” (pronoun): usually no article — Os sapatos são meus.
  • At the start of a sentence as pronoun: keep the article — O meu é azul.

With article vs without — nuance

Eles são meus filhos.
They are my children. (stating the relationship; no special contrast)
Eles são os meus filhos.
THEY are my children. (pointing out / distinguishing these particular kids)
Esta casa é minha. · Esta casa é a minha.
This house is MINE (ownership). · THIS one is mine (picking it out of a group).

When the article is often dropped

  • After ser with light emphasis: Eles são nossos vizinhos.
  • Possessive after the noun: Um amigo meu vai almoçar comigo.
  • With este/esta + possessive: Esta minha casa é antiga.
  • Vocative / addressing someone: Meu amigo, calma!
Brazilian Portuguese drops the article more often. In EP, when in doubt with a noun, prefer o meu / a minha… — it sounds natural.
Seu / sua can mean his, her, their, or your (formal). Context (or clarifying dele / dela) resolves ambiguity.
