Possessives — meu or o meu?

European Portuguese often puts a definite article before possessives: o meu livro. Sometimes the article drops. Both can be correct — the difference is emphasis and structure.

Default EP pattern
  • Before a noun: usually article + possessive — as minhas chaves, o nosso carro.
  • Standing alone as “mine / yours”: usually no article — Os sapatos são meus.
  • At the start of a sentence as pronoun: keep the article — O meu é azul.

With article vs without — nuance

Eles são meus filhos. · Eles são os meus filhos.
They are my children. · THEY are my children. (emphasis)
Esta casa é minha. · Esta casa é a minha.
This house is mine. · THIS one is mine.
Tip: Brazilian Portuguese drops the article more often. In EP, when in doubt with a noun, prefer o meu / a minha….
Seu / sua can mean his, her, their, or your (formal). Context (or dele / dela) resolves ambiguity.
