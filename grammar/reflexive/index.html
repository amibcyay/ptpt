Reflexive verbs

Many verbs add -se in the infinitive when the subject acts on themselves: lavar-se (to wash oneself). The reflexive pronoun (me, te, se…) matches the subject.

B1

Forms

Eu lavo-me. · Tu levantas-te cedo. · Ela veste-se. · Nós sentamo-nos.
Hyphen when enclitic; mesa alone in EP: sentamos-nos.

Common reflexive verbs

  • chamar-se — to be called · Como te chamas?
  • sentir-se — to feel · Sinto-me bem.
  • deitar-se — to lie down · Deito-me às onze.
  • lembrar-se de — to remember
  • esquecer-se de — to forget
Some verbs are always reflexive in Portuguese but not in English (queixar-se de — to complain). Learn them as phrases.
