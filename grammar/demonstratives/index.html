Demonstratives — isto, isso, este, esse…

Portuguese points to things with distance (near speaker, near listener, far from both) and with either a noun (variable forms) or no noun (isto / isso / aquilo).

Two groups
  • Variable — este/essa/aquele… agree with the noun (gender + number). Used before a noun or as pronouns.
  • Invariable (neuter) — isto, isso, aquilo never change and never sit before a noun. They replace the whole idea (“this thing”, “what you said”).

Three distances

DistanceVariable (masc. sg.)Neuter pronounTypical use
Near the speakereste livroistothis · here · now / very recent
Near the listener / just mentionedesse livroissothat · there (you) · what you said
Far from bothaquele livroaquilothat over there · distant time

With a noun vs without

Este livro é meu. · Essa caneta é minha. · Aqueles preços são altos.
This book is mine. · That pen is mine. · Those prices are high.
Isto é um absurdo. · Isso não é uma boa ideia. · O que é aquilo?
This is absurd. · That is not a good idea. · What is that (over there)?

Once you name the thing (livro, ideia), use the variable form that matches gender and number. For a whole situation with no noun, use isto / isso / aquilo.

Contractions (very common)

Demonstratives merge with de and em:

de + …em + …
deste, desta, destes, destas, distoneste, nesta, nestes, nestas, nisto
desse, dessa, desses, dessas, dissonesse, nessa, nesses, nessas, nisso
daquele, daquela, daqueles, daquelas, daquilonaquele, naquela, naqueles, naquelas, naquilo
Nisto não há problema. · Por isso não vim. · Nada disso!
There’s no problem with this. · That’s why I didn’t come. · No way!

European Portuguese vs Brazilian

In everyday European Portuguese, este/esta/isto are normal for “this” near the speaker. In much of Brazil, esse/essa/isso cover a wider range — so you may hear patterns that differ from EP textbooks.

EP: Passas-me estes dois vestidos, por favor?
Could you hand me these two dresses? (EP — near the speaker)
Memory hook: Speaker = este/isto. Listener / just said = esse/isso. Distant = aquele/aquilo. No noun → neuter (isto, isso, aquilo).
