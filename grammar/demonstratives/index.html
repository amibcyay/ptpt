Demonstratives — isto, isso, este, esse…

Portuguese points to things with distance (near speaker, near listener, far from both) and with either a noun (variable forms) or no noun (isto / isso / aquilo).

A2

Two groups
  • Variable — este/essa/aquele… agree with the noun. Used before a noun or as pronouns.
  • Invariable (neuter) — isto, isso, aquilo never change and never sit before a noun.

Este livro é meu. · Essa caneta é minha. · Isto é um absurdo.
This book is mine. · That pen is mine. · This is absurd.

Contractions (very common)

Nisto não há problema. · Por isso não vim. · Nada disso!
There's no problem with this. · That's why I didn't come. · No way!

European Portuguese vs Brazilian

In everyday EP, este/esta/isto are normal for “this” near the speaker. In much of Brazil, esse/essa/isso cover a wider range.

EP: Passas-me estes dois vestidos, por favor?
Could you hand me these two dresses? (near the speaker)
Tip: Memory hook: Speaker = este/isto. Listener = esse/isso. Distant = aquele/aquilo.
