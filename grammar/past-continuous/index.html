Past continuous (EP)

Ongoing action in the past uses estar (imperfect) + a + infinitive in European Portuguese.

B1

Pattern: estava / estavas / estava… + a + infinitive
Estava a chover quando chegaste. · Eles estavam a jantar.
It was raining when you arrived. · They were having dinner.
O que estavas a fazer ontem às oito?
What were you doing yesterday at eight?

Contrast with pretérito perfeito for a completed event: Estava a dormir quando o telefone tocou.
