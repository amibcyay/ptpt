Preposition pairings

Many Portuguese verbs and adjectives need a fixed preposition before their complement (regência). Learn the pairing with the word — don’t translate prepositions one-by-one from English.

Classic starter
  • gostar de — to like (+ noun / infinitive)
  • precisar de — to need
  • estar a + infinitive — to be doing (EP continuous)

Same verb, different preposition = different meaning

falar

falar com alguém — talk with someone · falar de algo — talk about something

pensar

pensar em — have on your mind · pensar de — have an opinion about
Estou a pensar em ti. · O que pensas desta ideia?

estar

estar a ler — be reading · estar em casa — be at home · estar com fome / dores — be hungry / in pain

esperar

esperar (+ infinitive) — hope · esperar por — wait for
Espero chegar a tempo. · Espero por ti.

ir

ir a — go to (often brief visit) · ir para — go to (destination / stay) · ir de — go by (transport)
Vou ao supermercado. · Vou para casa. · Vou de comboio.

After de / em / a, articles often contract: gosto do café (= de + o), estou na escola (= em + a), vou ao médico (= a + o). See articles & gender.
Don’t force English prepositions: “think about” is often pensar em, not pensar sobre (which exists but is less everyday for “on my mind”).
