Compound tenses

Compound tenses combine an auxiliary (ter or sometimes haver) with a past participle. The pretérito perfeito composto is especially common in EP for recent or continuing relevance.

B2

Structure: auxiliary (conjugated) + past participle (agrees in gender/number only with ter when direct object clitic precedes — advanced)

Pretérito perfeito composto

Tenho comido menos. · Tem chovido muito. · Havemos trabalhado o dia todo.
Often “have been …-ing” or recent past with present relevance.

Futuro composto (brief)

Teremos acabado antes das oito. · Assim que tiveres chegado, avisa.
Future perfect / action completed before another future point.

Ter vs haver as auxiliary

Ter is default for most verbs. Haver appears in formal or set patterns (há havido, têm havido). See haver page.
