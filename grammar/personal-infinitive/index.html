Personal infinitive

The infinitivo pessoal inflects for person — unique to Portuguese. It appears after prepositions and in certain subordinate clauses.

B2

Add endings to the infinitive: falar → falares (tu), falarmos (nós)…

After prepositions

Para ires à festa, precisas de bilhete. · Antes de falarmos, ouve isto.
For you to go… · Before we talk…

Same forms as future subjunctive (regular verbs)

For regular verbs, personal infinitive endings match future subjunctive — context tells them apart.

É melhor ires agora. · Quando eles chegarem, começamos.
