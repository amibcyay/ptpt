Conjunctions

Conjunctions link words, phrases, or clauses. A handful of coordinators (e, mas, ou, porque) and subordinators (quando, se, que) cover most A2 writing and speech.

A2

Coordinating — join equal parts

WordUseExample
eandChá e café
masbutQuero ir, mas estou cansado
ouorSegunda ou terça?
porquebecauseFico em casa porque chove
portanto / por issoso / thereforeChove, por isso fico

Subordinating — introduce a clause

  • quando — when: Quando chegares, telefona.
  • se — if: Se chover, ficamos em casa. (often triggers subjunctive later)
  • que — that: Penso que ele vem. (indicative after many verbs)
  • embora — although: Embora chova, vou sair.
Tip: At B2 you'll meet more subordinators and the subjunctive after many of them — see Subjunctive overview.
