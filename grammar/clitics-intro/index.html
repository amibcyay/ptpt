Clitic pronouns — introduction

Object pronouns (me, te, o, a, lhe…) usually attach to the verb in European Portuguese. In simple affirmative sentences they come after the verb — EP default is enclisis.

A2

Starter set
Direct objectExample
me, te, o/a, nos, vos, os/asEle vê-me. · Ajudo-te. · Compro-o.

After the verb (affirmative)

Diz-me a verdade. · Telefono-te amanhã. · Ela convida-nos.
Hyphen in writing; pronounced as one unit.

Before the verb (proclisis)

Negative sentences and many “magnetic” words pull the pronoun before the verb:

Não me digas isso. · Nunca te vi. · Quando me telefonas?
Não, nunca, question words, subordinating conjunctions…
A sentence cannot start with a clitic in standard EP — *Me ajuda is ungrammatical; say Ajuda-me or Podes ajudar-me?
Tip: EP prefers proclisis more than Brazilian PT in some contexts, but affirmative declaratives still use enclisis: Ele disse-me.
