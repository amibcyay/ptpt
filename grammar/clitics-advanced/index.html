Clitic pronouns — advanced

Beyond me/te, EP uses 3rd-person clitics o, a, os, as (direct) and lhe(s) (indirect). Pronouns can merge (de-lo) and appear in mesoclisis with future/conditional.

B2

Third-person direct objects

Vi-o ontem. · Comprei-a na loja. · Conheço-os de vista.
O/a/os/as replace specific nouns (must agree).

Indirect: lhe / lhes

Dei-lhe o livro. · Escrevi-lhes um email.

Merging pronouns

Dá-mo = dá-me + o (give it to me) · Vou dar-ta (I'll give it to you)
Written with hyphen; order rules apply.

Mesoclisis (future / conditional)

Dar-te-ei a resposta. · Dir-lhe-íamos amanhã.
Pronoun inside future/conditional form — formal; often avoided in speech.
Tip: In speech, rephrase to avoid mesoclisis: Vou dar-te a resposta.
