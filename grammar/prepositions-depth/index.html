Prepositions in depth

The core prepositions de, em, a, com drive location, origin, and direction. They merge with articles into contractions you'll use every day.

B1

Core prepositions

Prep.Typical senseExample
defrom, ofVenho de Faro
emin, atEstou em casa
ato, at (movement/time)Vou a pé · Às três horas
comwithVou contigo

Questions with prepositions

In EP the preposition often stays at the end (or before the verb in formal style):

De onde és? · Com quem vais? · Para onde vamos?
Where are you from? · Who are you going with? · Where are we going?
