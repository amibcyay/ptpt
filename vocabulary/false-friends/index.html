False friends

Words that look or sound like English but mean something different in European Portuguese. Learn these early to avoid embarrassing mistakes.

Context matters. Some words below have a related English meaning in specific contexts — the “trap” column is what EP speakers usually understand first.

information · problem · university · hospital — cognates, but learn article + pronunciation.
